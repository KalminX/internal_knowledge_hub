Synchronized Document Lifecycle & Instant Invalidation

Cloud connectors preserve file SHA-256 signatures, timestamps, and permissions. When documents change or are archived in Google Drive or SharePoint, the synchronization worker executes immediate pruning of obsolete vector embeddings to prevent orphan chunk contamination.
